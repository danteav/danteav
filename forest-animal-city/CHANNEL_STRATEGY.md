# WOBBLY HOLLOW — Channel Strategy

## The Show
**Name:** Wobbly Hollow
**Format:** Nonverbal cartoon — no dialogue, no words, ever
**Runtime:** 10–15 minutes per episode
**Audience:** Kids 2–8, parent co-viewing
**Global reach:** Zero language barrier — no dubbing, no subtitles needed

---

## YouTube Channel Setup

### Channel Details
- **Channel name:** Wobbly Hollow
- **Handle:** @WobblyHollow
- **Category:** Kids & Family
- **Content setting:** Mark ALL videos as "Made for Kids" (COPPA compliance)
  - This automatically disables comments and personalized ads

## Upload Strategy

### Launch Plan
Do NOT launch with one episode. Build a small library first so new visitors have more to watch.

| Phase | Action |
|---|---|
| **Pre-launch** | Produce 3–4 episodes before going live |
| **Launch week** | Upload episodes 1 and 2 on the same day (back-to-back binge) |
| **Week 2** | Episode 3 |
| **Week 3+** | One episode per week, same day, same time |

### Upload Schedule
- **Day:** Thursday (kids watch heavily Friday–Sunday; Thursday upload gives the algorithm time to push it)
- **Time:** 10:00 AM in your target timezone
- **Frequency:** 1 episode per week minimum

### When to Batch Release
If you get 2–3 episodes ahead in production, batch-release the first few. New visitors convert better when there's more to watch. After that, return to weekly cadence.

## Thumbnails

### The Formula (never change this)
1. **One character face** — huge, front and center, minimum half the frame
2. **The emotion** — must read at thumbnail size (2 inches on a phone screen)
3. **The obstacle visible** — show the problem, not the solution
4. **Bright background** — solid or simple scene, never cluttered
5. **No episode numbers** — irrelevant for standalone format
6. **Minimal or no text** — the face does the work

### Per-Episode Thumbnail Direction
| Episode | Character | Emotion | Obstacle shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Too Big | Bruno | Frustrated confusion | Enormous chair + tiny door |
| The Cake | Bea | Terror/determination | Wobbling 5-tier cake |

### Thumbnail Consistency Rules
- Same art style every episode
- Same font if text is used
- Same character proportions
- Viewers should recognize a Wobbly Hollow thumbnail instantly

## YouTube Shorts Strategy

Every episode has 2–3 moments perfect for Shorts (under 60 seconds, vertical 9:16).

### How to use Shorts
- Post 2 Shorts per episode release week
- Shorts drive channel discovery — algorithm pushes them to non-subscribers
- Shorts funnel to full episodes

### Best Shorts moments per episode
| Episode | Short 1 | Short 2 |

## Monetization Roadmap

### Short term (0–1K subscribers)
- Nothing yet — focus on building the library and consistency

### YouTube Partner Program (1K subs + 4K watch hours)
- Apply for monetization
- Note: "Made for Kids" content earns lower CPM than general content
- BUT: kids content gets enormous watch time (kids rewatch obsessively)
- Watch time is more valuable than views for the algorithm

### Growing (10K+ subscribers)
- **Merchandise:** Characters are strong enough — plush toys, stickers, t-shirts
  - Sprout stuck in gate = perfect plushie
  - Bruno fail pose = perfect sticker
- Print-on-demand services (Printful, Redbubble) require zero inventory

### The Big Opportunity — Streaming Licensing
Nonverbal animated kids content is the most licensable format in the world:
- Netflix Kids, Apple TV+, Disney+, Amazon Kids+, Peacock Kids
- Zero localization cost (no dubbing = no barrier to international deal)
- These platforms actively seek completed nonverbal series
- 8 episodes = enough to pitch a "series" — not just a pilot

**Path:** Build to 50K+ subscribers, document your watch time and retention stats, pitch to streaming acquisitions teams or use an agent/manager.

## Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Average view duration** | Shows if kids are finishing episodes (rewatch = algorithm love) |
| **Click-through rate (CTR)** | Tells you if thumbnails are working (aim for 5%+) |
| **Impressions** | How often YouTube is recommending you |
| **Returning viewers %** | Core audience health |
| **Traffic source: YouTube Kids** | Once content is in Kids app, this tells you the size of that audience |
