# CANOPY CITY
## Episode 6: "The Race"
**Runtime:** 10–15 minutes
**Lead family:** The Hopgoods (Pip) + The Fennics (Zara)
**No dialogue. No words. Pure visual storytelling.**
**Emotion arc:** Excitement → Competition → Jealousy → Anger → Frustration → Embarrassment → Disappointment → Respect → Pride → Joy
**Values:** Fairness · Respect · Perseverance · Sportsmanship · Honesty

---

### THE OBSTACLE
Pip Hopgood challenges the neighborhood kids to a big race through Wobbly Hollow. He sets the course, he writes the rules, and he expects to win — because he always wins. But when Pip starts bending his own rules to stay ahead, the race falls apart, and putting it right means doing something much harder than winning: admitting you were wrong.

---

## BEAT 1 — SETUP (0:00–1:30)

**EXT. MOSSY HOLLOW LANE — MORNING**

*Pip has posted a handmade sign on the lamp post: a drawing of a running figure with a big trophy. He has drawn an arrow pointing to himself. He is very pleased with this sign.*

*Kids gather: Bernie, Zara, River, Dot, Toby, Fern, Bella.*

*PIP presents the course with great ceremony — pointing along the route: down Mossy Hollow Lane, around the fountain, through the market, up the hill to the Great Oak, back down, finish at the lamp post.*

*EMOTION: Pip — authority and excitement. This is his race.*

*EMOTION: Everyone else — enthusiasm. This is going to be fun.*

*The starting line is drawn in chalk. Gerta is handed a starting flag (she agreed to wave it). Gerta waves the flag at approximately tortoise speed.*

*RACE BEGINS.*

---

## BEAT 2 — ATTEMPT & FAIL (1:30–5:00)

**THE RACE — FIRST HALF**

*The racers sprint. It's genuinely fun and chaotic — Toby trips on his shoelaces immediately, gets up, keeps going. Bella drops Nutsy at the start line, comes back for her, keeps going (determined, not upset). Bernie runs with enormous effort — he's not fast but he's absolutely committed.*

*ZARA is keeping pace. She's surprisingly fast.*

*PIP is in front — but only just. He glances back at Zara. His jaw tightens.*

---

**THE FOUNTAIN**

*At the fountain, you're supposed to go around it. The rule is: keep the fountain on your left.*

*Pip gets there first — but Zara is right behind him. In a split-second decision, Pip cuts inside the fountain instead of going around.*

*He glances back. Did anyone see?*

*DOT saw. She was watching from behind. She stops. She points at where Pip went. She looks at the other racers.*

*EMOTION: Dot — her quiet certainty. She knows what she saw.*

*ZARA catches up. She goes around correctly. She ends up neck-and-neck with Pip despite doing the right thing.*

*Pip's jaw tightens more.*

---

**THE MARKET**

*Through the market — you're supposed to go through the stall lane, not around the outside.*

*Pip reaches the market. Zara is alongside him. He swerves — outside the stalls, the forbidden shortcut, faster.*

*He gets ahead.*

*EMOTION: Pip — a flash of guilt crosses his face. Then he pushes it down and runs.*

*ZARA runs through the stall lane correctly. She's now behind but only by a few steps.*

---

**THE HILL**

*Up the hill. Pip is gassing — he's pushed too hard. Zara is steady. She starts to gain.*

*Pip looks back. He sticks out a foot — not to trip Zara, more of a desperate instinct — and immediately pulls it back.*

*EMOTION: Pip — horror at himself. He didn't do it. But he almost did. He knows what he almost did.*

*Zara crests the hill and comes alongside him on the descent.*

*EMOTION: Pip — pure panic. He sprints.*

---

**THE FINISH**

*Pip crosses the finish line first. He raises his arms. He's done it.*

*The other racers arrive. Bernie is last (he is completely delighted with himself regardless). Dot arrives quietly. She does not cheer for Pip.*

*Everyone looks at Pip.*

*EMOTION: Pip — waiting for the celebration. It doesn't feel right. He knows why.*

*He looks at Dot. Dot looks back at him.*

*Zara, catching her breath, sees the look between them.*

*EMOTION: Pip — the guilt he pushed down is back. Bigger now. It's not going away.*

---

## BEAT 3 — OTHERS NOTICE (5:00–7:00)

*Everyone is catching their breath. Pip holds the chalk trophy drawing he'd prepared. It feels wrong in his hands.*

*BERNIE is completely happy — he finished! He is showing everyone his totally last-place finish like it's a gold medal. He has no ego about this at all.*
*VALUE: Perspective — joy in participation, not just winning.*

*CLOVER arrives (she didn't race, she was watching). She saw everything. She stands near Pip. She doesn't say anything. She just stands there.*

*EMOTION: Pip — the specific discomfort of someone who knows and has someone who knows standing next to them.*

*PIP looks at the trophy drawing. He looks at Zara. Zara is sitting on the curb writing in her notebook — she's already moved on.*

*But Pip hasn't.*

*He looks at the course. He looks at the fountain shortcut. He looks at the market shortcut.*

*EMOTION: The full weight of it. The win is hollow. He put it there himself.*

*VALUE: Honesty working on him — the inner discomfort of a conscience doing its job.*

---

## BEAT 4 — TOGETHER (7:00–11:30)

**PIP MAKES IT RIGHT**

*Pip walks over to Zara.*

*She looks up from her notebook.*

*Pip points to himself. He mimes the fountain — going around, then slicing inside. He points to himself again. He holds up two fingers: two shortcuts.*

*He reaches out and takes the chalk trophy drawing from under his arm. He holds it out to Zara.*

*EMOTION: Zara — surprised. She looks at the drawing. She looks at Pip. She looks at Dot (who gives her nothing, as usual).*

*Zara shakes her head. She takes out her notebook. She draws two figures crossing a finish line side by side. She points to both of them.*
*VALUE: Zara's generosity — she doesn't want the win handed to her. She wants a real race.*

*Pip looks at the drawing.*

*He nods. A slow nod.*

**THE RE-RUN**

*Just Pip and Zara. Gerta waves the flag again (at tortoise speed).*

*The other kids line the course, watching.*

*They run. This time, the course is followed exactly. At the fountain — Pip goes around, the long way. At the market — Pip goes through the stall lane, the right way.*

*It's close. Very close.*

*Zara wins. By one step.*

*EMOTION: Pip — stands there. A beat.*

*He walks over. He holds out his paw.*

*Zara shakes it.*

*VALUE: Sportsmanship. Losing honestly and gracefully.*

*EMOTION: Pip's face — not happy, but clean. The right kind of feeling. Pride in doing the right thing even though it hurt.*

---

*BERNIE immediately challenges both of them to a rematch where everyone runs backwards. The tension breaks — everyone laughs.*

*The afternoon becomes a series of increasingly silly races: backwards, hopping, carrying an egg on a spoon (Scout provides the egg and the spoon and the extremely detailed rules), eyes closed, crawling.*

*Pip wins exactly zero of these. He laughs after every single one.*
*VALUE: Joy in participation — the real point of a race.*

---

## BEAT 5 — PAYOFF & QUIET (11:30–15:00)

*End of the afternoon. Everyone tired. Sprawled on Mossy Hollow Lane in the late sun.*

*Pip is lying on his back looking at the sky. Zara sits nearby.*

*Zara opens her notebook. She shows him the drawing of two figures crossing the line together. She adds another detail: a small star on both figures.*

*VALUE: Recognition without hierarchy — both tried hard, both matter.*

*— Bella is showing Dot the drawings Dot made of the race. Dot never entered the race — she was drawing it the whole time. Bella points to one drawing — Dot's version of herself, notebook in hand, observing. She taps Dot on the shoulder and claps.*
*VALUE: Appreciating others' different strengths — Dot didn't run, but she contributed something irreplaceable.*

*— Toby is retying his shoelaces. He has tied them again incorrectly. Scout watches for a moment, then carefully shows him how — slowly, step by step. Toby tries. Gets it. His face.*
*VALUE: Patience in teaching. Kindness without condescension.*

*— Gerta still has the flag. She is slowly, methodically folding it. She has been folding it for the last hour.*

*Pip picks up the chalk trophy drawing from the ground — Zara didn't take it. He adds a detail: two stick figures on the podium, side by side.*

*He props it against the lamp post where the race announcement was.*

*FADE OUT.*
