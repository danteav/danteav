# CANOPY CITY
## Episode 4: "Stuck"
**Runtime:** 10–15 minutes
**Lead family:** The Hopgoods (Sprout + all siblings)
**No dialogue. No words. Pure visual storytelling.**
**Emotion arc:** Happiness → Surprise → Worry → Frustration → Panic → Anger → Laughter → Relief → Love
**Values:** Patience · Helping Others · Consideration · Perseverance · Respect

---

### THE OBSTACLE
Sprout Hopgood (age 4, tiny, fearless) has gotten her head gently, completely, and thoroughly stuck in the garden gate's decorative iron loop. She is fine. She is cheerful about it. Every attempt to free her makes something else go wrong. By the time all four families are involved, the gate has also become stuck, the garden hose has gotten involved, and old Gerta's flower pot is somehow part of the situation.

---

## BEAT 1 — SETUP (0:00–1:30)

**EXT. HOPGOOD WARREN — MORNING**

*Bright sunny day. The Hopgood family garden — a wildflower explosion of color behind an ornate iron garden gate with a large decorative circular loop in the center.*

*SPROUT toddles out of the house in her little dungarees, heading for the garden. She looks at the gate loop. She looks at her head. The loop is clearly about the same size as her head.*

*She tilts her head sideways. She threads it through the loop.*

*It fits perfectly.*

*She looks very pleased with herself.*

*She tries to pull back out.*

*It does not come back out.*

*EMOTION: Sprout — mild puzzlement. She tries again. Still stuck. She looks left. She looks right. She tries once more.*

*She looks at the camera. She shrugs with her whole body.*

*She reaches into her pocket and pulls out a cracker. She eats it calmly while stuck in the gate.*

---

## BEAT 2 — ATTEMPT & FAIL (1:30–5:00)

**CLOVER ARRIVES**

*Clover comes out of the house with Sprout's hat, stops at the sight, does a full-body freeze, and then calmly assesses.*

*She grips Sprout's shoulders gently. She pulls — slowly, carefully.*

*Sprout's cheeks puff out as her head doesn't move. The gate rattles.*

*Clover tries angling Sprout's head the way she came in. Sprout's ear folds awkwardly. Clover reverses. Sprout's ear unfolds. Nothing moved.*

*EMOTION: Clover — focused concentration becoming careful worry. Not panicking. Methodical. She sits back and studies the loop.*
*VALUE: Patience — Clover doesn't rush or yank.*

---

**PIP AND DOT ARRIVE**

*Pip sees the situation and immediately tries to pull Sprout from the other side — with enthusiasm.*

*CLOVER holds up a hand sharply. Stop. Pip stops.*

*Pip crosses his arms, frustrated. He points to Sprout. Points back through the gate.*
*EMOTION: Pip — frustration at being told to stop when he just wants to fix it.*

*Clover shakes her head. Points to the iron loop. Mimes: the angle matters.*

*Pip thinks. Nods. Slower this time, he puts both hands on either side of Sprout's head and tries a careful tilt-and-pull.*

*Sprout's ears are now both folded up around the loop.*

*EMOTION: Everyone stares at Sprout's ear situation. Sprout crosses her eyes trying to look at her own ears. She giggles.*

*Dot produces her sketchbook. She draws the gate loop and Sprout's head from above — a top-down diagram. She holds it up and traces the path with her pencil. She points to: tilt left, then forward.*
*VALUE: Consideration — Dot uses her observation skill to actually help.*

*They try Dot's method. Sprout's left ear almost clears — but the loop has ALSO gotten stuck, somehow, on Sprout's collar button.*

*Wider problem than before.*

*EMOTION: Collective groan (silent but visible — everyone's shoulders drop at once).*

---

**SCOUT ARRIVES WITH MAGNIFYING GLASS**

*Scout investigates the situation extremely closely with the magnifying glass. He examines the loop. He examines Sprout's head. He examines the collar button.*

*He stands up. He has found the problem. He makes a great show of locating the exact catch point — yes, there, that tiny ridge on the inside of the loop that the collar button has snagged on.*

*He produces a tiny stick. He reaches in to lever the button free.*

*The stick snaps.*

*Scout stares at the two halves of the stick.*
*EMOTION: Scout — embarrassed deflation. He pockets the halves with dignity.*

---

**THE GARDEN HOSE INCIDENT**

*Someone (TOBY FENNIC, who has appeared) suggests using the garden hose to make everything slippery enough to slide Sprout free.*

*This is not a good idea. Everyone can see it's not a good idea.*

*They try it anyway.*

*The hose is turned on. Sprout gets drenched. The gate gets drenched. Clover gets drenched. The collar button is now very slippery and has moved to an entirely new snagged position.*

*Sprout — soaking wet, still stuck, still cheerful — blows a bubble with the water dripping down her face.*

*EMOTION: The siblings look at each other. Wet. Tired. Not defeated but definitely at capacity.*
*VALUE: Perseverance — they're frustrated but nobody has given up or gotten mean.*

---

## BEAT 3 — OTHERS NOTICE (5:00–7:00)

*RUBY comes around the corner on her bicycle, postal bag over shoulder, making her rounds. She skids to a stop at the scene: all five of her children, soaking wet, crowded around the garden gate where Sprout's head is stuck, with a garden hose snaking through the scene.*

*EMOTION: Ruby's full-body freeze. Slow turn. Eyes taking in the whole scene.*

*She leans her bicycle against the fence. She takes off her postal bag. She rolls up her sleeves.*

*She crouches in front of Sprout. She looks at the loop. She looks at the collar button from the other side. She puts her cheek against the gate to see the angle from underneath.*

*She reaches very carefully through the loop and, with two fingers, unbuttons Sprout's collar button.*

*She tilts Sprout's head — left, forward, slight right.*

*Sprout's head slides free.*

*Complete silence.*

*Sprout looks at her own hands. Pats her own head. Looks up at everyone. Grins.*

*EMOTION: Eruption. Clover slumps against the fence in relief. Pip punches the air. Dot closes her sketchbook contentedly. Scout examines Sprout's head with the magnifying glass for any damage (there is none). Toby takes a bow for some reason.*

*RUBY holds Sprout at arm's length, looks her over completely, then pulls her into a hug.*
*EMOTION: Love. Real, full, relief-soaked love.*

---

## BEAT 4 — TOGETHER (7:00–11:30)

**EXT. HOPGOOD WARREN — CLEANUP**

*The garden is a mess. The hose has flooded a flower bed. The gate is muddy. The garden path is slippery.*

*Ruby looks at the chaos. Takes a breath.*

*The Fennic family is passing the gate (Finn, Faye, Zara, Toby). They see the mess. They stop.*

*Finn looks at the flooded flower bed. He looks at the gate drainage. He sees the problem — the hose left the drain blocked. He wordlessly crouches down and unblocks the drain.*
*VALUE: Helping others without being asked.*

*Faye produces a mop from absolutely nowhere. Nobody questions this.*

*Zara and Clover work together sweeping the path. They find a rhythm quickly.*

*BERNIE BERRYMORE arrives (he followed the hose chaos from down the street). He's too big for the detailed work, so he carries the heavy soggy flower bed soil in a bucket without anyone asking him to.*
*VALUE: Finding how YOU can help specifically.*

*GERTA TORTOISE watches from her front step across the lane. She disappears inside. She reappears very slowly carrying a plate of biscuits. She sets them on the garden wall.*
*VALUE: Consideration — Gerta's small reciprocal kindness.*

*When the garden is restored, everyone stands looking at it. It's actually tidier than before the whole incident.*

---

## BEAT 5 — PAYOFF & QUIET (11:30–15:00)

*Everyone sits on the garden wall eating Gerta's biscuits. Sprout sits in the middle, completely unbothered, eating the largest biscuit.*

*— Clover splits her biscuit and gives half to Scout (who ate his too fast).*
*VALUE: Sharing.*

*— Pip had been teasing Scout earlier about the broken stick. Now he hands Scout a better stick he found — a proper one, with a forked end. Scout's eyes go wide.*
*VALUE: Making it right without being asked — consideration.*

*— Ruby looks at all five of her kids. She counts them with one paw, one by one. She gets to Sprout. She gets to five. She exhales slowly. She puts an arm around Clover, who was the responsible one all morning.*
*VALUE: Recognition — Clover's patience and leadership quietly acknowledged.*

*— Dot has drawn the entire event in her sketchbook. The last drawing: Sprout's head in the gate, completely serene, eating a cracker. Dot shows it to Sprout. Sprout laughs so hard she falls off the wall (she's fine).*

*Final shot: The garden gate, flower bed restored, damp but sunny. A tiny cracker crumb still sitting in the decorative loop.*

*FADE OUT.*
