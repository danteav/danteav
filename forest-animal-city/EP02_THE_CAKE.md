# CANOPY CITY
## Episode 2: "The Cake"
**Runtime:** 10–15 minutes
**Lead family:** The Berrymores (Bea)
**No dialogue. No words. Pure visual storytelling.**
**Emotion arc:** Excitement → Pride → Worry → Frustration → Anger → Embarrassment → Honesty → Relief → Joy → Love
**Values:** Honesty · Patience · Respect · Consideration · Helping Others

---

### THE OBSTACLE
Bea has baked a spectacular five-tier celebration cake for Mayor Hedgewick's town anniversary party. It must travel from the Honey Loaf Bakery all the way across Wobbly Hollow to the Great Oak Town Hall — on foot, on a cart, down cobblestone streets, past the fountain, up a small hill. The cake is tall, wobbly, and covered in delicate frosting flowers. Every bump is a disaster waiting to happen.

---

## BEAT 1 — SETUP (0:00–1:30)

**INT. HONEY LOAF BAKERY — MORNING**

*Warm yellow light. Flour in the air. BEA steps back from her worktable and looks at her masterpiece: five tiers of layered cake, each decorated with tiny frosted flowers, a miniature Wobbly Hollow skyline piped in icing around the middle tier, and a small sugar Mayor Hedgewick on top (enormous hat included). It is extraordinary.*

*EMOTION: Bea clasps her hands together. Eyes crescent with happiness. Pure pride.*

*She carefully loads it onto a flat cart with a handle. She looks at the route through the window — cobblestone street, fountain square, uphill to Town Hall.*

*She looks at the cake. Back at the street. She wraps the cart handle with both paws and takes a very deep, very careful breath.*

*EMOTION: Quiet, focused determination. She's done hard things before.*

---

## BEAT 2 — ATTEMPT & FAIL (1:30–5:00)

**EXT. MOSSY HOLLOW LANE — THE FIRST COBBLESTONE**

*Bea pushes the cart out the door. The first cobblestone bump sends a tiny wobble through all five tiers. Bea freezes. Watches it settle. Exhales.*

*She pushes more slowly. Agonizingly slowly. She's bent at the waist, eyes level with the cake, watching every layer.*

*A SQUIRREL child races past on a scooter — whoosh — the air current from the child's speed tilts the top tier slightly.*

*Bea freezes. The top tier rocks. Rocks. Settles.*

*EMOTION: Terror. Eyes huge. Both paws flying up to almost-catch it. Then relief so intense she has to hold the cart for support.*

*She looks at the squirrel child — the child doesn't know what they nearly caused.*
*VALUE: Bea takes a breath. She doesn't scold. She waves politely instead.*
*VALUE: Patience and respect — choosing kindness when you're stressed.*

---

**THE FOUNTAIN SQUARE**

*The fountain square has a mossy cobblestone lip around the fountain — a small raised ring, maybe two inches high. A perfect tiny obstacle.*

*Bea eyes it. She has a plan: go slow, angle the wheels.*

*She goes slow. She angles the wheels.*

*The front wheel catches the lip anyway. The cart lurches. The cake sways dramatically — left — right — LEFT—*

*Bea lunges, both arms around the cake, face pressed against the side, holding it still.*

*A beat. She is hugging a cake in the middle of the town square. Citizens stop to watch.*

*EMOTION: Mortification. Cheeks bright red. Eyes dart left and right at the audience of onlookers.*
*VALUE: Embarrassment shown fully — real and relatable.*

*The cake settles. She lets go carefully. She straightens up. She gives the watching citizens a very dignified nod.*

*EMOTION: Dignity recovered. Just barely.*

---

**THE HILL**

*The small hill up to Town Hall looks steeper from the bottom than it did on the map.*

*Bea pushes. The cart is heavy. Halfway up, she slows. Three-quarters up, she's straining, leaning forward with everything she has, tiny steps.*

*The cart begins rolling back.*

*EMOTION: Panic — eyes wide. She digs her heels in.*

*She holds it. But she cannot go forward and she cannot hold this forever. She looks up the hill. Looks back down. Looks at the cake.*

*Her face does something complicated: frustration crumbles into something more honest — she needs help. She's not going to make it alone.*

*She sets the wheel brake (a small wooden wedge under the wheel) and straightens up. She looks around.*

---

## BEAT 3 — OTHERS NOTICE (5:00–7:00)

*CLOVER HOPGOOD appears at the top of the hill, coming the other direction, arms full of postal letters she's delivering for Ruby. She sees Bea at the bottom, sees the cart, sees the cake, reads the situation instantly.*

*She sets the letters down carefully in a neat pile against the wall.*

*She walks down to Bea. She doesn't wait to be asked. She positions herself at the back of the cart.*

*Bea looks at her. Something warm crosses her face — relief and gratitude.*

*VALUE: Helping others without being asked.*

*Together they push. The cart moves. Slowly, then steadily, they crest the hill.*

*At the top — FINN and ZARA are passing by with a wagon of supplies. Finn sees the situation, parks his wagon, and wordlessly holds the cart steady while Bea and Clover catch their breath.*

*VALUE: Consideration — Finn sees they're tired and just holds it without making it a big deal.*

---

## BEAT 4 — TOGETHER (7:00–11:30)

**EXT. GREAT OAK TOWN HALL — ALMOST THERE**

*The Town Hall door is visible. They're almost there — twenty feet away.*

*TOBY FENNIC (who has been perched on a ledge above, watching) spots a bee landing on the top tier of the cake. Just sitting there. On the sugar Mayor Hedgewick.*

*Toby's eyes go wide. He points urgently.*

*Everyone looks. The bee is investigating the sugar figure.*

*Bea sees it. EMOTION: Absolute frozen terror.*

*Finn reaches for a tool. Zara reaches for her notebook (she has a bee diagram). Clover slowly raises one hand.*

*The bee flies away on its own.*

*Everyone exhales.*

*They push the final twenty feet. The Town Hall door opens. A BADGER BUTLER holds it wide.*

*They wheel the cake inside and park it on the display table.*

*Bea steps back. The cake is perfect. Somehow, completely, impossibly perfect.*

*EMOTION: She covers her mouth with both paws. Eyes fill (just barely — not crying, just full). The tiny sugar Mayor Hedgewick on top is still standing.*

*EMOTION: Everyone looks at the cake, then at Bea, then back at the cake. A quiet shared pride — they all did this.*

---

*MAYOR HEDGEWICK (small, fussy, enormous hat) enters the hall and stops dead at the sight of the cake. His hat slowly slides off his head. He doesn't notice.*

*He looks at Bea. He places a tiny hedgehog paw over his heart and bows.*

*EMOTION: Bea — eyes crinkling, soft smile, small bow back.*
*VALUE: Mutual respect — given and received with grace.*

---

## BEAT 5 — PAYOFF & QUIET (11:30–15:00)

**INT. GREAT OAK TOWN HALL — THE PARTY**

*The party is underway. Wobbly Hollow citizens fill the hall. The cake is the centerpiece.*

*Bea watches from the side as the Mayor cuts the first slice — carefully, ceremonially. He offers the first piece to Bea. She shakes her head and points to Clover.*

*VALUE: Consideration — giving the honor to someone who helped.*

*Clover gets the first slice. Her ears shoot straight up with surprise and delight.*

*— Bea notices a small mouse child at the edge of the crowd, too short to see the cake. Bea lifts the child up so they can see it properly. The child's face.*
*VALUE: Consideration for the smallest in the room.*

*— Finn is trying to sneak a second slice. Faye catches him. He puts it back. She hands him half of hers.*
*VALUE: Sharing — with a knowing look that makes it funny.*

*— Toby is somehow already on his third slice. No one can explain this.*

*Final shot: The empty cake stand at the end of the party — just the sugar Mayor Hedgewick figure left standing on the plate. Someone has put a tiny crumb crown on his head.*

*FADE OUT.*
