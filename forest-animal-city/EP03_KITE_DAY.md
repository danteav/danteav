# CANOPY CITY
## Episode 3: "Kite Day"
**Runtime:** 10–15 minutes
**Lead family:** The Dawnleys (River + Fern)
**No dialogue. No words. Pure visual storytelling.**
**Emotion arc:** Excitement → Joy → Jealousy → Anger → Sadness → Guilt → Forgiveness → Inclusion → Triumph → Love
**Values:** Sharing · Inclusion · Forgiveness · Consideration · Honesty

---

### THE OBSTACLE
It's a perfect windy day in Canopy City — ideal for kites. River and Fern Dawnley have a beautiful new kite. But when River gets carried away showing off and accidentally wrecks Pip Hopgood's kite, the fallout threatens to ruin kite day for everyone — until honesty and a generous act of forgiveness set everything right.

---

## BEAT 1 — SETUP (0:00–1:30)

**EXT. WILLOWBROOK PARK — MORNING**

*Perfect blue sky. Strong steady wind bends the long grass and shakes the willow tree. It is an absolutely ideal kite day.*

*Kids are arriving from all directions — the Hopgood kids with a bright red kite, Zara and Toby with a yellow one, Bella with a small purple kite shaped like a butterfly.*

*RIVER and FERN arrive with the biggest kite of all — a beautiful blue-and-green diamond kite with a long tail. River is bouncing. Fern is holding it carefully with both hands.*

*EMOTION: Everyone — excitement and delight. Kites unfurling, strings unreeling, running starts.*

*Wide shot: a dozen kites rising into the blue sky over the park. Pure joy.*

---

## BEAT 2 — ATTEMPT & FAIL (1:30–5:00)

**EXT. WILLOWBROOK PARK — THE SHOW-OFF**

*River's kite is flying beautifully — high, steady, graceful. River is thrilled. A little TOO thrilled.*

*River starts showing off: spinning the kite in circles, doing figure-eights, flying it in impossibly close dips near the ground and then shooting it back up.*

*EMOTION: Pride tipping into showing-off. The grin is a little too wide. The chest a little too puffed.*

*Fern watches from nearby. Her expression: mild worry. She knows River.*

*The other kids watch in admiration. PIP watches with growing jealousy — jaw tightening, arms crossing. His red kite is fine, but it's not doing any of that.*
*EMOTION: Jealousy clearly shown — Pip's narrowing eyes, the slow cross of arms, glancing between his kite and River's.*

---

*River does the biggest trick yet — a sweeping low arc that brings the kite SCREAMING across the park at head height—*

*—directly into the string of Pip's red kite.*

*The strings tangle. Both kites spin. River yanks back — wrong choice — the strings pull taut and PIP'S KITE TEARS. A long rip through the fabric. The red kite crumples and falls.*

*Silence in the park.*

*Pip stares at his kite on the ground. Then at River.*

*EMOTION: Pip — shock → jaw trembling → eyes fill → turns away. Real sadness, not a tantrum.*

*River — horror. Mouth open. Both hands over face. The showing-off is gone instantly.*

*EMOTION: River — guilt, visible and complete. The body language collapses: shoulders cave, head drops, arms hang.*

---

**THE FALLOUT**

*Clover comes to Pip. She crouches next to him. She doesn't say anything — just sits with him. He leans against her.*
*VALUE: Consideration — just being present is enough.*

*River stands apart. Fern appears next to River — not touching, not fixing, just there.*

*River looks at the torn kite. Looks at Pip. Looks at the ground.*

*EMOTION: River's face works through something — the temptation to make excuses, to point at the wind, to say it was an accident—*

*River doesn't. Instead, River walks toward Pip.*

*Pip looks up. Jaw still set. Arms still crossed.*

*River picks up the torn kite. Holds it out — an offering. Then points up — their kite. Then to Pip. River is offering their own kite.*
*VALUE: Honesty and genuine accountability — not just an apology gesture but a real giving-up-of-something.*

*Pip stares. Clover watches.*

*Pip shakes his head. Pushes the torn kite back toward River.*

*EMOTION: River — fresh hurt. Fern puts a gentle hand on River's shoulder.*

---

## BEAT 3 — OTHERS NOTICE (5:00–7:00)

*The mood of the whole park has shifted. Kites are flying but the energy is subdued. Kids keep glancing over at the standoff.*

*BELLA is nearby, holding her small purple butterfly kite. She has been watching everything.*

*She walks over to Pip. She holds out her kite. Her butterfly kite — her favorite thing.*

*VALUE: Sharing — Bella is giving something she loves, not something spare.*

*Pip looks at Bella. She's so small. She's offering so earnestly.*

*EMOTION: Pip's crossed arms loosen — just a little.*

*DOT appears from the other side and sits down next to the torn kite. She opens her sketchbook. She starts drawing something.*

*ZARA has been watching with her notebook. She writes something, shows it to River — it's a diagram of how the strings crossed. It's not blame, it's explanation. It shows it could have happened to anyone in those wind conditions.*
*VALUE: Consideration — Zara is trying to help River feel less alone in the guilt.*

*River looks at the diagram. Looks at Pip.*

---

## BEAT 4 — TOGETHER (7:00–11:30)

*DOT looks up from her sketchbook. She's been drawing on the torn kite fabric itself — a detailed picture of the whole park, all the kids, all the kites. It's beautiful.*

*She holds it up.*

*Everyone gathers to look. Even Pip.*

*Pip looks at the drawing. His torn kite — now it has something on it. Something real.*

*FERN has quietly disappeared and come back with a small sewing kit from her nature bag (of course she has one). She holds it up with a questioning look at Pip.*

*Pip looks at the torn fabric. At Dot's drawing. At Fern's sewing kit. At River.*

*A long beat.*

*Pip nods.*

*Everyone works together on the kite repair — a wonderful visual sequence:*
*— Fern sews the main tear with neat precise stitches*
*— Dot adds a little more to the drawing around the edges*
*— Zara reinforces the frame junction with thread*
*— Bella holds the fabric taut*
*— River holds the frame steady, carefully, the most careful River has ever been*
*— Pip watches at first — then reaches in and holds one corner himself*

*VALUE: Inclusion — Pip joining is the emotional hinge of the whole episode.*

*When it's done, the kite is repaired — and Dot's drawing covers the whole face of it. It's better than before. More personal. More theirs.*

*Pip picks it up. Looks at it.*

*He looks at River.*

*River reaches out a paw. An apology that asks nothing.*

*EMOTION: Pip — the crossing of arms fully releases. He takes River's paw.*
*VALUE: Forgiveness — freely given, no conditions.*

*River's face: relief so intense it's almost collapse.*

---

**THE FLYING**

*They all run together. The repaired kite goes up — Dot's drawing visible from below, swooping across the blue sky.*

*River and Pip hold the string together, side by side.*

*EMOTION: Joy. Clean, uncomplicated, earned joy.*

*Bella's butterfly kite goes up alongside it. Then the yellow one. Then the rest.*

*Wide shot: a sky full of kites over the park. The repaired kite — largest of all, most beautiful — at the center.*

---

## BEAT 5 — PAYOFF & QUIET (11:30–15:00)

*Wind has gentled. Kites are drifting lazily. Kids lie on the grass looking up.*

*River and Pip are side by side. River is clearly about to do something with the kite string — another trick — and glances at Pip first. A question in the look.*

*Pip raises an eyebrow. River mimes the looping trick slowly, carefully. Asks again.*

*Pip thinks. Then: a slow nod. But he holds up one finger — one loop, not five.*

*River grins. Does one loop, perfectly controlled.*
*VALUE: Respect — asking before acting. Accepting the limit.*

*— Fern is packing away her sewing kit. Clover comes over and bows slightly — a thank you. Fern dips her head back.*
*VALUE: Respect — quiet acknowledgment.*

*— Bella gets her butterfly kite back from the child she'd lent it to while the repair was happening. The child didn't want to give it back. Bella crouches to the child's level, points to the sky where all the other kites are — then to her own kite — then to the child. She gives it back.*
*VALUE: Sharing — giving when you don't have to.*

*Final wide shot: afternoon light. The park. Kites drifting. Everyone together.*

*The repaired kite — Dot's drawing across its face — dips once in a gust, then steadies and rises.*

*FADE OUT.*
