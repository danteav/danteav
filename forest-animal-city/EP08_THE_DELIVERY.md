# CANOPY CITY
## Episode 8: "The Delivery"
**Runtime:** 10–15 minutes
**Lead family:** Ruby Hopgood + Finn Fennic + Bruno Berrymore
**No dialogue. No words. Pure visual storytelling.**
**Emotion arc:** Busy → Chaos → Collision → Anger → Guilt → Honesty → Forgiveness → Cooperation → Triumph → Joy
**Values:** Honesty · Forgiveness · Consideration · Helping Others · Respect · Patience

---

### THE OBSTACLE
Ruby's urgent postal delivery, Finn's remote-controlled test vehicle carrying a fragile component, and Bruno's heavily-loaded supply cart all converge at the same intersection at the same moment — with spectacular consequences. The crash damages all three missions. Rebuilding requires honesty about who caused what, and forgiveness to move forward.

---

## BEAT 1 — SETUP (0:00–1:30)

**THREE PARALLEL INTRODUCTIONS**

*Split-screen opening: the same sunny morning, three different starting points.*

**RUBY** — post office, dawn. Her postal bag is enormous. She has a special red-flagged urgent package prominently on top. She checks her route map, snaps it shut, mounts her bicycle, and sets off. Fast.*

**FINN** — workshop, slightly later. He has built a small remote-controlled cart — his test vehicle — carrying a large glass component (clearly fragile: extra padding, bright orange "FRAGILE" label drawn on, three warning arrows). He has a remote control. He is extremely focused. He sends the cart out ahead, walking alongside with the remote.*

**BRUNO** — Build & Supply, same time. His large supply cart is piled high with lumber, tools, bags of sand. He's making a delivery to the new building on Copper Lane. He's cheerful, waving at neighbors, not watching the road quite carefully enough.*

*WIDE SHOT: the map of Canopy City. Three animated lines show each route. They all pass through — THE INTERSECTION of Mossy Hollow Lane and Acorn Alley.*

*They're all heading there at the same time.*

---

## BEAT 2 — THE COLLISION (1:30–3:30)

**THE INTERSECTION**

*Ruby comes around the corner fast — she's running late.*
*Finn's remote cart trundles into the intersection from the left — he's around the bend, out of sight.*
*Bruno's supply cart rolls in from the right — he's waving at a shopkeeper.*

*All three arrive at once.*

*Ruby swerves — hits Finn's cart — the glass component wobbles—*
*Bruno's cart (he finally looked forward) brakes but catches the edge of Ruby's bicycle—*
*Finn's remote cart spins — the glass component tips — falls—*
*Ruby's urgent package bounces from her bag—*
*Bruno's sand bags split—*

*A cloud of sand, a musical clash of cart-on-cart, Ruby sliding sideways off her bicycle (she's fine), and then—*

*Silence.*

*The three of them stand in a sandy, messy intersection staring at the damage:*
*— Finn's glass component: broken. He holds up the two pieces.*
*— Ruby's urgent package: the red-flagged parcel is in the sand, its wrapper torn.*
*— Bruno's cart: one wheel cracked, load shifted, half the sand gone.*

*EMOTION: Stunned silence. Then simultaneously — Finn looks at Bruno, Bruno looks at Ruby, Ruby looks at Finn.*

*EMOTION: Anger rising in all three. Brows down. Jaws set. Fingers begin to point in three different directions.*

---

## BEAT 3 — THE BLAME (3:30–5:30)

*All three pointing at each other. The geometry is contested:*
*— Finn: points at Ruby (she hit my cart)*
*— Ruby: points at Bruno (his cart blocked me)*
*— Bruno: points at Finn (his cart came from nowhere)*

*EMOTION: Genuine anger — not exaggerated, real. They're all losing time. The component is broken. The package is damaged. The wheel is cracked.*

*Citizens have gathered to watch from a respectful distance.*

*The pointing continues. Heat is rising.*

*Then: GERTA TORTOISE is slowly crossing the intersection — has been the whole time, apparently. Everyone stops pointing. They wait for Gerta to cross.*
*VALUE: Even in anger, basic respect doesn't disappear.*

*Gerta reaches the other side. The three look at each other.*

*The anger deflates — not into resolution, but into something more complicated. Because they're not entirely wrong about each other. But they're not entirely right either.*

*EMOTION: The hard feeling of knowing you're partly at fault.*

---

*DOT HOPGOOD has appeared from somewhere. She taps Ruby's arm and holds up her sketchbook — she drew the whole thing from a window above. She shows the sequence: Ruby's speed, Finn's cart around the blind corner, Bruno's inattention.*

*She shows it to all three. One by one.*

*EMOTION: Each face reads the drawing. Each face shows the same thing: recognition. And the specific discomfort of seeing your own role in something.*
*VALUE: Honesty arriving via evidence — sometimes it takes seeing it clearly.*

*Finn looks at his drawing — his cart was around the blind corner where nobody could see it. He looks at Ruby.*

*Ruby looks at her drawing — she was going too fast. She looks at Bruno.*

*Bruno looks at his drawing — he wasn't watching. He looks at Finn.*

*They all look at each other.*

---

## BEAT 4 — TOGETHER (5:30–11:30)

**HONESTY AND FORGIVENESS**

*Finn goes first. He picks up the broken glass component, looks at it, sets it down. He looks at Ruby. He puts his paw on his chest and bows slightly — I take responsibility for my part.*
*VALUE: Honesty — acknowledging your contribution.*

*Ruby looks at him. She takes a breath. She reaches out and shakes his paw.*
*VALUE: Forgiveness — meeting honesty with grace.*

*Bruno does the same — points to himself, then to the mess, bows to both of them. He's clearly sorry.*

*Ruby shakes his paw too.*

*Then Ruby points to herself — her speed — and bows to them both.*
*VALUE: Mutual accountability — everyone owns their part.*

*Three paws meet in the center. A beat.*

*EMOTION: Not instant joy — something quieter. The release of a fight that didn't have to become permanent.*

---

**THE REBUILD**

*Now: practical problem. The glass component is broken. The package is torn. The wheel is cracked. And all three missions are still urgent.*

*They look at the problems. They look at each other.*

*Finn examines the glass component. It's broken in two — cleanly. His eyes go to his tool belt. He has adhesive. He begins carefully piecing it back together.*

*Bruno looks at his cracked wheel. He has replacement parts on his own supply cart (naturally). He gets to work.*

*Ruby re-wraps the package. She doesn't have tape. She looks around.*

*BELLA BERRYMORE appears with a ribbon (she happened to have it). She helps Ruby tie the package closed.*
*VALUE: Community — help appears when you're honest about needing it.*

*BERNIE helps Bruno with the wheel (holding it steady while Bruno bolts it).*
*VALUE: Helping others.*

*CLOVER helps Finn hold the component pieces while the adhesive sets.*

*Nobody organized this. Everyone just stepped in.*

---

**THE MISSIONS RESUMED**

*The component is repaired — not perfect, but solid.*
*The package is resealed — slightly crumpled, but respectable.*
*The wheel is replaced.*

*Finn gently loads the repaired component back onto his cart. He looks at it. He nods.*

*Ruby checks her watch. She is now very late. She looks at the distance to her destination.*

*Bruno — without being asked — loads Ruby's bicycle into his cart and points: I'll take you to the delivery point faster. She can make up the time.*
*VALUE: Consideration — seeing someone's problem and solving it without waiting to be asked.*

*Ruby stares at him. Then nods, grabs her postal bag, climbs onto Bruno's cart alongside her bicycle.*

*Finn walks his repaired component cart alongside.*

*The three of them travel together to the delivery destination — a somewhat chaotic, very slow convoy, but together.*

*EMOTION: Companionable quiet. The warmth after a fight resolved.*

---

**THE DELIVERY**

*The urgent package arrives. It's late but it's there. The recipient (a small hedgehog who was clearly anxious) holds it to their chest in relief.*

*Ruby's face: satisfaction. The job is done.*

*EMOTION: The specific pride of completing something despite everything going wrong.*

---

## BEAT 5 — PAYOFF & QUIET (11:30–15:00)

*The intersection where the crash happened. It's been cleaned up — the sand swept, the debris gone. Someone has put a small potted flower on the corner lamp post.*

*(Nobody will ever know if it was Finn or Bruno or Ruby who put it there. That's the point.)*

*— Finn brings the repaired component to his workshop. He sets it in place. He tests it — it works. He exhales. He looks at it for a moment, then puts a small label on it: a drawing of three paws meeting.*
*VALUE: Remembering the people who helped you get there.*

*— Ruby finishes her route — last stop. She's exhausted. She sits down on a bench. She opens her postal bag for the last time. Right at the bottom: a honey bun, wrapped in paper. From Bea? From Bruno? There's no note.*
*VALUE: Anonymous kindness — the gift with no credit attached.*

*— Bruno wheels his supply cart to the building site and makes his delivery. On the way back, he passes the intersection. He looks at it.*

*He produces a bucket of chalk from his cart. He draws a large colorful arrow on the ground at the corner of the blind turn — warning: cart coming — so nobody has a repeat of this morning.*
*VALUE: Consideration for future strangers — making something safer for people you'll never meet.*

*Final shot: the chalk arrow at the intersection. Finn's cart goes around it. He sees it. He stops. He looks at it.*

*He smiles.*

*He takes out his own chalk and draws a second arrow on the other approach.*

*The intersection is now clearly marked. Two chalk arrows, two different hands.*

*FADE OUT.*
