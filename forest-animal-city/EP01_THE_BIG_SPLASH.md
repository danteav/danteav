# CANOPY CITY
## Episode 1: "The Big Splash"
**Runtime:** ~22 minutes
**Positive Message:** When we share our strengths, we're stronger together than any of us could be alone.

---

### COLD OPEN (0:00 – 1:30)

*WIDE ESTABLISHING SHOT — Wobbly Hollow at dawn. The sun rises slowly behind the ancient trees, painting the sky peach and gold. We drift through the city: rope bridges catching morning light, bakery chimneys puffing cinnamon-scented smoke, a squirrel newspaper carrier tossing papers onto doorsteps.*

*AERIAL SHOT descends toward MOSSY HOLLOW LANE — a curved cobblestone street lined with cozy homes. We stop at a row of four houses, each distinct.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
*(warm, gentle)*
This... is Wobbly Hollow. Home to over a thousand animals, twelve bakeries, one very opinionated mayor, and the four most interesting families on Mossy Hollow Lane.

*QUICK CUTS of each family waking up:*

*— BERRYMORE DEN: Bruno snoring thunderously, Bea already at the stove, Bella hugging Nutsy, Bernie rolling out of bed and immediately bumping his head on the ceiling. He gives a thumbs-up.*

*— FENNIC TOWNHOUSE: Finn already up, surrounded by half-finished gadgets, talking to himself. Faye appears, removes a wrench from his coffee cup without a word, hands it back.*

*— HOPGOOD WARREN: Chaos. Pure chaos. Ruby juggling five breakfasts at once while Scout uses a magnifying glass on his toast. Sprout sits in the sink. Clover runs in, counts all five kids, breathes.*

*— DAWNLEY STILT-HOUSE: Dax painting quietly. River already halfway up a bookshelf. Fern sits reading with a cup of tea, deeply peaceful — until River falls off the shelf.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
Today, like every day, would be perfectly ordinary in Wobbly Hollow.

*DRAMATIC THUNDER RUMBLE. A single raindrop hits the camera lens.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
Mostly.

*SMASH to TITLE CARD: CANOPY CITY — "The Big Splash" — in bright, bouncy letters with a raindrop dripping off the "S."*

---

### ACT ONE: THE STORM (1:30 – 7:00)

**EXT. MOSSY HOLLOW LANE — MORNING**

*The sky has turned a deep purple-grey. Wind stirs the leaves. BERNIE BERRYMORE (10, large brown bear cub, wearing a backpack three sizes too big) and ZARA FENNIC (9, sleek red fox kit, notebook tucked under her arm) walk together toward school.*

**BERNIE**
I'm just saying — I have a GREAT plan for the Spring Festival booth. Giant trebuchet. We launch acorn pies. People catch them in their mouths.

**ZARA**
*(without looking up from her notebook)*
Five structural problems with that. Want me to list them?

**BERNIE**
...Maybe later. Hey, does the sky look weird to you?

*ZARA looks up. A wall of dark clouds is rolling in fast — unnaturally fast.*

**ZARA**
That's a lot of cumulonimbus for a Tuesday.

*The first raindrop. Then ten. Then a DOWNPOUR. Kids scatter. Bernie grabs Zara and they sprint under the awning of The Honey Loaf Bakery.*

**INSIDE THE HONEY LOAF BAKERY**

*BEA BERRYMORE slides the front door closed against the wind. Outside, rain hammers the windows. FINN FENNIC runs in from across the street, drenched, shaking water like a dog.*

**FINN**
*(gasping)*
The shop sign! I just repainted it! *(looks at his now-waterlogged reflection in the window)* Ah. Well. I'm fixing that too.

**BEA**
Finn, get in here. I'll make tea.

*RUBY HOPGOOD bursts in, postal bag clutched to her chest, absolutely soaked, looking cheerful anyway.*

**RUBY**
I've still got forty letters to deliver!

**BEA**
Ruby, you'll float away!

**RUBY**
*(already pulling out soggy letters)*
The 4:00 PM delivery window is a commitment, Bea.

*DAX DAWNLEY enters carefully, holding an umbrella that has turned completely inside out.*

**DAX**
*(looking at the useless umbrella)*
I feel this is a metaphor for something.

*They all crowd around the bakery window watching the rain. The Mossy Hollow Lane FOUNTAIN is already overflowing.*

**ZARA**
*(pointing, alarmed)*
The drainage grate. Look — it's blocked.

*CLOSE ON: The main drainage grate in the center of Mossy Hollow Square is completely clogged with autumn leaves and debris. Water is pooling fast.*

**BERNIE**
We can go unblock it!

**ZARA**
In this? We'd be swept away.

**FINN**
*(perking up)*
I have an idea! I have a drainage hook at the shop — extended reach, telescoping handle —

**BEA**
Finn. You're also going to have to wait.

*The water level RISES. Within minutes, Mossy Hollow Square — the heart of the neighborhood — is flooded ankle-deep. Water laps at shop doorsteps.*

**BERNIE**
*(hushed)*
Oh no. The Spring Festival...

*Everyone looks at each other. The Spring Festival setup was supposed to happen TODAY. Booths, banners, tables — all of it planned for Mossy Hollow Square.*

**RUBY**
*(quietly)*
Mayor Hedgewick is going to announce the festival canceled.

*As if on cue, a MEGAPHONE crackles outside. Through the rain they see MAYOR HEDGEWICK (small, fussy hedgehog, enormous ceremonial hat now completely waterlogged) standing on the steps of the Great Oak Town Hall across the square, speaking through a cone.*

**MAYOR HEDGEWICK (V.O.)**
*(tinny, through rain)*
Attention citizens! I am hereby officially declaring the Spring Festival... POSTPONED until further notice! The square is flooded! There's nothing to be done!

*Beat.*

**BERNIE**
...There's something to be done.

*He looks at Zara. She looks at her notebook.*

**ZARA**
*(slowly)*
Maybe there is.

---

**INT. HONEY LOAF BAKERY — CONTINUOUS**

*The four adults and the kids who've gathered (Bernie, Zara, Clover and Pip Hopgood, Fern and River Dawnley, who arrived via the back alley) crowd around a bakery table covered in flour. Zara has flipped to a blank page.*

**ZARA**
Okay. Problem: the square is flooded because the drainage grate is blocked. Solution: unblock the grate. Challenge: the water's too deep and fast for any one of us to reach it safely.

**RIVER**
I can climb anything! I'll get there!

**FERN**
River, there's nowhere to climb. It's flat ground.

**RIVER**
*(deflating)*
...Oh yeah.

**FINN**
My telescoping hook can reach the grate. But I need to get to my shop to get it, and my shop is across the square.

**CLOVER**
*(studying the room)*
The awnings. If we connect the bakery awning to the butcher shop awning to the lamp post... we could make a covered walkway. Stay dry and above the water.

*Everyone looks at Clover, surprised.*

**CLOVER**
*(suddenly self-conscious)*
I mean. Probably. Maybe.

**BEA**
That's a brilliant idea, Clover.

**CLOVER**
*(quietly pleased)*
It's just geometry.

**BERNIE**
Okay! So — Finn gets to his shop. Then what?

**FINN**
The hook is telescoping, but someone still has to stand near the grate to guide it. In the water.

*Silence. Everyone looks down at the flooding square.*

**RUBY**
I'm a postal carrier. I walk in everything.

**FINN**
Ruby, it's not sa—

**RUBY**
*(already tying up her postal bag)*
I walk in everything. That's my strength. That's what I do.

**DAX**
*(thoughtful)*
And I can guide from higher ground. I have the best eye for angles — I measure proportions for paintings every day. I can call out directions to Ruby.

*BERNIE has been bouncing on his heels.*

**BERNIE**
And I can carry stuff! I'm the biggest! *(looks at Bella, who wandered in from the back)* Bella, you stay safe inside—

**BELLA**
*(holding up Nutsy)*
Nutsy says we can help too.

**BEA**
*(kneeling to Bella's level)*
You know what Mama needs? The best helper in the bakery. Because when everyone comes back cold and wet — and they will — I need enough honey buns for the whole neighborhood.

*Bella considers this seriously.*

**BELLA**
...Okay. Nutsy's good at measuring.

*BEA smiles, looks up at the adults.*

**BEA**
I'll be here. Hot food waiting. You all go fix our square.

---

### ACT TWO: THE PLAN IN ACTION (7:00 – 15:00)

**EXT. MOSSY HOLLOW — AWNING OPERATION**

*Rain still hammering down. CLOVER directs her siblings with intense focus.*

**CLOVER**
Pip — hold that corner! Scout, stop touching the rope with your magnifying glass— Scout!

**SCOUT**
I'm examining the rope FIBERS, Clover. For weakness.

**CLOVER**
It's fine! It's rope!

**TOBY FENNIC** (6, absolutely soaking wet, having the time of his life) appears from nowhere.

**TOBY**
I'm helping!

**CLOVER**
Where did you come from?!

**TOBY**
*(already climbing the lamp post)*
I'm very fast!

*Despite the chaos, the awning-bridge slowly takes shape. BERNIE uses his size to stretch the canvas across the gap, grunting with effort. River ties the knots with practiced speed — all that climbing means great knot work.*

**RIVER**
*(tying the last knot)*
Done!

**BERNIE**
*(testing it)*
That's... actually really sturdy.

**RIVER**
*(grinning)*
Trees don't let go of things. Neither do I.

*The covered walkway stretches from the bakery to the lamp post to the entrance of Finn's Fix-It Shop. Finn shimmy-runs across it, nearly slips once, catches himself on a support rope.*

**FINN**
*(from inside his shop, shouting out)*
I've got it! Coming out!

*He emerges with the TELESCOPING HOOK — a long, clever tool that extends in segments. But it's also heavier than he expected.*

**FINN**
Ah. Right. Physics.

*BERNIE lumbers over and takes one end without a word. They carry it together.*

**FINN**
*(surprised)*
Thank you, Bernie.

**BERNIE**
That's what I'm for.

---

**EXT. MOSSY HOLLOW SQUARE — THE GRATE**

*The flood is real — water halfway up Bernie's shin. The DRAINAGE GRATE is visible below the surface, about fifteen feet from the edge of the square.*

*DAX stands on the elevated fountain base (the only dry spot), eye-level above the water, studying the angle like he's composing a painting.*

**DAX**
Ruby — if you come in at about a forty-degree angle from the lamp post, the current is weakest there. Veer left of the big pebble.

**RUBY**
*(stepping in, gasping at the cold)*
FORTY DEGREES! Got it! Which way is left?!

**DAX**
...Your left!

*Ruby wades out. The kids on the edges hold their breath. Sprout has appeared on Bernie's shoulders because nobody saw it happen.*

**SPROUT**
*(cheerfully)*
Go, Mama! Go!

**RUBY**
*(waving)*
I'm okay! It's just really cold!

*FINN extends the telescoping hook, guiding it toward Ruby. But the current keeps tugging the end sideways.*

**ZARA**
*(suddenly)*
Wait — if we angle it diagonally from two points, the current can't push it. Like a triangle. Triangles are stable!

*She grabs one side of the hook. BERNIE grabs the other. Together they form a brace.*

**ZARA**
Ruby — guide the tip!

*Ruby grabs the tip of the hook under the water and steers it down toward the grate. We see the hook catch on the debris clog.*

**FINN**
*(straining)*
On three — pull! One, two—

**ALL FOUR**
THREE!

*A massive SUCKING SOUND. The debris comes loose. A WHOOSH — water starts draining fast, spiraling down the now-clear grate. The flood level drops. And drops. And drops.*

*Everyone freezes.*

*The square is still wet — but the water is draining. Rapidly.*

*RUBY looks down at the clearing cobblestones. She laughs — a real, surprised, delighted laugh. And everyone else does too.*

**SPROUT**
*(from Bernie's shoulders, arms raised)*
WE DID IT!

**TOBY**
*(doing a victory flip off the lamp post, landing perfectly)*
YEAH!

**CLOVER**
*(trying to stay composed, failing, grinning)*
...Yeah. Yeah we did.

*FERN has been standing quietly at the edge. She's staring at the sky. The purple clouds are... breaking up. Through the gap, sunlight pours in — golden, warm, landing on the wet cobblestones of Mossy Hollow Square and turning everything into a glittering, gorgeous reflection.*

**FERN**
*(barely a whisper)*
Oh.

*She starts humming — just a little. Something quiet and beautiful. Nobody's ever heard her do this before.*

*River looks at her, surprised. Fern notices, stops. Looks embarrassed.*

**RIVER**
*(gently)*
Don't stop. That was nice.

*A beat. Fern hums again, a little louder.*

---

### ACT THREE: SAVING THE FESTIVAL (15:00 – 20:30)

**EXT. MOSSY HOLLOW SQUARE — AFTERNOON**

*The square is WET but clear. Puddles reflect the sky. The sun is fully out now, making everything gleam. The kids stand looking at the empty festival space — no booths, no banners, no setup.*

**BERNIE**
The festival was supposed to start at five. That's... *(checks his watch)* ...four hours.

**ZARA**
We'd need booths, banners, food stalls, the stage—

**PIP**
That's impossible.

**BERNIE**
*(looking at Zara)*
Is it?

*Long pause.*

**ZARA**
*(writing in her notebook)*
Probably not.

*NEW ENERGY. The adults are pulled in by the kids' momentum.*

**FINN**
I have spare lumber at the shop. Pre-cut. Was meant for a different job but—

**BRUNO**
*(appearing suddenly, massive, cheerful, having just heard everything)*
I have MORE lumber. And I have eight kinds of brackets.

**FINN**
...We might actually do this.

*BEA emerges from the bakery with a TRAY of honey buns.*

**BEA**
Fuel first. Plan second.

*Brief honey bun interlude. Everyone eating, warming up. The quiet moment before the storm of activity.*

**DAHLIA DAWNLEY** (arriving from the library direction, arms full of colorful fabric) —

**DAHLIA**
I heard what happened. I brought bunting. And streamers. And fourteen strings of lanterns.

**DAX**
*(lovingly)*
Of course you did.

---

**MONTAGE — THE FOUR-HOUR FESTIVAL BUILD**
*Set to bright, bouncy orchestral music with a ticking-clock energy.*

- BRUNO and FINN work side by side — Bruno's strength, Finn's precision. First argument (Finn: "Three millimeters matters!" Bruno: "It's a BOOTH, Finn!"), then mutual respect, then a perfect booth.

- CLOVER organizes the Hopgood kids into a banner-painting assembly line. DOT paints the best mushroom she's ever drawn. SCOUT labels everything with his magnifying glass.

- RIVER and BERNIE drag tables out of storage — River climbs to hook the banner ropes to high branches; Bernie holds the ladder steady.

- FERN and BELLA arrange flowers and lanterns around the square, Bella with Nutsy "helping." Fern hums quietly and Bella hums along — they don't need to talk.

- RUBY is everywhere at once, somehow, delivering things to people and checking things off a list she wrote on her postal notepad.

- DAX paints a WELCOME TO THE SPRING FESTIVAL banner in large, gorgeous letters — animals stop to stare as they pass.

- ZAra builds a small GAMES BOOTH with Toby's "assistance." It works despite him.

*End of montage: the square is transformed. Lanterns glow warm in the early evening light. Booths line the cobblestones. The banner spans the square. It's beautiful — maybe more beautiful than planned, because it was built with urgency and love.*

*MAYOR HEDGEWICK (still slightly damp) walks out of Town Hall, expecting to see an empty square. His eyes go wide.*

**MAYOR HEDGEWICK**
But I... I declared... I said postponed—

**BEA**
*(gently)*
With respect, Mayor, you declared it postponed. We just... didn't listen.

**MAYOR HEDGEWICK**
*(looking around in wonder)*
Who did all this?

*He looks at the four families: wet, tired, covered in paint and sawdust and flour, grinning at each other.*

**MAYOR HEDGEWICK**
*(quietly, genuinely moved)*
Well. I suppose I'll have to reschedule the cancelation.

---

### ACT FOUR: THE SPRING FESTIVAL (20:30 – 22:00)

**EXT. MOSSY HOLLOW SQUARE — EVENING**

*The festival is alive. Citizens fill the square — otters, badgers, owls, chipmunks, hedgehogs. Music plays. Games run. The Honey Loaf booth has the longest line.*

*We drift through the happiness in slow, golden-lit shots:*

— Bella and Sprout spinning in circles holding hands, dizzy and giggling.
— Bruno and Finn at a shared booth (Build & Fix, together), arguing happily about something, then high-fiving.
— Ruby watching her kids play, sitting down for the first time all day, letting out a long exhale. Bea sits next to her and hands her tea. No words needed.
— River dragging Fern to the dance area. Fern says no three times, then yes.

*At the center of the square, MAYOR HEDGEWICK climbs a small stage. He taps the microphone awkwardly.*

**MAYOR HEDGEWICK**
Citizens of Wobbly Hollow! This morning, our square flooded. And I — your mayor — declared the festival impossible.

*Murmurs.*

**MAYOR HEDGEWICK**
I was wrong. Because I forgot that impossible just means it hasn't been done yet. And in four hours, the families of Mossy Hollow Lane proved exactly that. So this festival — improbably, wonderfully — belongs to all of you.

*Applause. BERNIE and ZARA stand next to each other.*

**BERNIE**
You know, your triangle idea really saved it. The hook thing.

**ZARA**
Your carrying saved it. The whole thing was physical.

**BERNIE**
We're a pretty good team.

**ZARA**
*(writing in her notebook)*
Noted.

*FERN, at the edge of the crowd, hums — then sings, just a single phrase of something wordless. RIVER looks at her.*

**RIVER**
You should do that on the stage sometime.

**FERN**
*(pause)*
...Maybe.

*The lanterns glow warm above the square. The forest breathes around the city. Fireflies appear at the edges of the trees — early this year, as if they came for the festival.*

*Wide shot: the whole square, glowing, full, alive.*

*SLOW ZOOM OUT through the canopy, past the treetops, to the stars beginning to appear.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
That night, in Wobbly Hollow, nobody remembered whose idea it had been to start fixing things. Because it didn't matter. It never does.

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
What mattered was that they'd each brought what they had — and what they had, together, turned out to be exactly enough.

*Final shot: The four front doors of Mossy Hollow Lane, lantern-lit. One by one, lights go off. The forest settles. The creek murmurs.*

*FADE TO BLACK.*

*END CARD: "THE END" in warm letters. A small animated honey bun bops into frame, looks at the camera, and waves.*

---

## END CREDITS

*Credits roll over the festival still running — the animals dancing, playing games, Toby somehow on top of a booth, Scout examining a firefly. A warm, loving slice of the world continuing.*

*POST-CREDITS TAG:*
*Sprout is still in the square alone. Mama has forgotten her. SPROUT looks around, perfectly calm.*

**SPROUT**
*(to a passing firefly)*
Hello. I live here now.

*Ruby runs back into frame at full speed.*

**RUBY**
SPROUT!

**SPROUT**
*(already running to her, arms out)*
MAMA!

*They crash hug. Blackout.*
