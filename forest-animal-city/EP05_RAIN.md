# CANOPY CITY
## Episode 5: "Rain"
**Runtime:** 10–15 minutes
**Lead family:** All four families equally
**No dialogue. No words. Pure visual storytelling.**
**Emotion arc:** Calm → Surprise → Scramble → Frustration → Empathy → Generosity → Warmth → Joy
**Values:** Sharing · Consideration · Respect · Helping Others · Patience

---

### THE OBSTACLE
A sudden, unexpected rainstorm hits Wobbly Hollow on what was supposed to be a perfect sunny day. Every family is caught mid-activity with something precious, fragile, or urgently needed — and nobody has enough shelter or cover for everything. The solution requires every family to share what they have.

---

## BEAT 1 — SETUP (0:00–1:30)

**EXT. CANOPY CITY — SUNNY MORNING**

*Perfect day. Warm sun. Not a cloud in the sky.*

*QUICK CUTS of each family mid-activity:*
*— Dax is outside painting a large canvas — his best work yet, propped on an easel in the garden*
*— Bea has set out dozens of fresh-baked goods on the bakery's outdoor display tables to cool*
*— Ruby has just laid out ALL of the family's clean laundry to dry on the line*
*— Finn has spread out every piece of a complex machine he's repairing across the workshop yard — tiny parts everywhere*

*Everything is outside. Everything is vulnerable.*

*One cloud appears. Small. Dark. Moving fast.*

*EMOTION: Nobody notices.*

*The cloud gets bigger.*

*EMOTION: Still nobody notices.*

*The cloud is now very large and very purple and directly overhead.*

*One raindrop hits the camera lens.*

*Then: DOWNPOUR.*

---

## BEAT 2 — ATTEMPT & FAIL (1:30–5:00)

**FOUR SIMULTANEOUS SCRAMBLES**

*The episode now cross-cuts rapidly between four families in parallel crisis:*

---

**DAX AND THE PAINTING**

*Dax turns from his canvas at the first drop. He looks at the sky. He looks at his painting. He grabs the canvas and runs for his front door — but the door is locked and the key is inside. He pats every pocket. No key.*

*He holds the painting over his head as a shield — but it's too big and the rain hits everything around him.*

*FERN throws open an upstairs window, drops the key — it falls in a puddle. Dax fishes it out, slippery, tries to get it in the lock — drops it again.*

*EMOTION: Dax — genuine distress. This is his best work. His face shows real fear of losing it.*

---

**BEA AND THE BAKING**

*Bea sprints out of the bakery. She's trying to carry all the cooling trays in at once — too many. She grabs as many as she can. The first load collapses. Honey buns everywhere.*

*She scoops them up. Runs in. Runs back out. Grabs more. Rain is soaking everything.*

*BERNIE runs out to help — slips on the wet step — slides — crashes into a display table — more buns go everywhere.*

*EMOTION: Bea — frustration cresting. She puts her paws on her hips and looks at the bun-covered wet yard.*
*EMOTION: Bernie — flat on back, covered in buns, looks up at the rain.*

---

**RUBY AND THE LAUNDRY**

*Ruby sprints for the laundry line. She starts yanking clothes off — but the line is long and the clips are tight and the rain is making everything heavy.*

*She gets half the laundry. Sprout has helpfully unclipped the other half and it's now blowing across the garden.*

*Scout chases a shirt with his magnifying glass. Pip chases a pair of trousers, full sprint. Dot stands in the rain watching a sock float past — she calmly picks it up.*

*EMOTION: Ruby — a moment of quiet anger. She stops. Breathes. This is too much for one family.*

---

**FINN AND THE PARTS**

*Finn runs into the yard. Tiny machine parts everywhere — in rain, they'll rust.*

*He grabs his jacket and lays it over one cluster. He grabs his hat and covers another. He grabs a flowerpot and upturns it over a third cluster.*

*He looks at the remaining fifteen clusters of parts. He looks at himself — no more clothes.*

*He stands in the rain, glasses steaming, staring at unprotected parts.*
*EMOTION: Frustration → the particular helplessness of having no more solutions.*

---

## BEAT 3 — OTHERS NOTICE (5:00–7:00)

*DAX, still locked out, painting held above his head, notices BEA in the bakery yard trying to rescue goods. He sees: the bakery awning — a big, wide awning that would cover a lot of ground.*

*He holds up his painting. Points to the awning. A question.*

*Bea understands immediately. She cranks the awning out as wide as it goes. It covers the bakery yard AND the alley next to it.*

*Dax runs his painting under it. Safe. He exhales.*

*EMOTION: Gratitude. He bows to Bea, soaking wet.*
*VALUE: Consideration — Bea shared her shelter without hesitation.*

*Dax then looks across the lane and sees FINN standing helplessly in the rain next to his parts.*

*He runs over. He takes off his painting smock — already ruined — and lays it over the parts.*
*VALUE: Giving what you have, even when it costs you something.*

*RUBY appears in the lane, arms full of wet laundry, kids scattered behind her. She sees Bea's awning.*

*Bea waves her over — come, come — ushering everyone under.*
*VALUE: Sharing space and shelter.*

*Under the bakery awning: Bea, Dax, Ruby, all the Hopgood kids, Finn, his parts covered by Dax's smock. Cramped. Warm. Everyone dripping.*

---

## BEAT 4 — TOGETHER (7:00–11:30)

**UNDER THE AWNING**

*The rain hammers down. Everyone is squished together but dry.*

*EMOTION: The initial scramble-energy settles into something slower — shared warmth. People look at each other. Small relieved smiles.*

*But: the laundry is still out there. Ruby looks at it. Twelve items on the line, soaking.*

*She moves to go get it — Bea puts a hand on her arm. Not worth it. Wait.*

*Ruby hesitates. She looks at the shirts. She sits back.*
*VALUE: Patience — letting go of what can't be fixed right now.*

---

*CLOVER is doing a headcount under the awning. All her siblings: present. Then she notices — the Fennic kids (Zara and Toby) are not here. She looks out into the rain.*

*Zara is sheltering in a doorway half a block down, notebook clutched to her chest. Toby is next to her, soaking, pretending he's not.*

*Clover ducks out into the rain. She runs to them. She waves: come on, come on. She takes Zara's arm. She runs back. They all make it under the awning.*

*Now it's really cramped. But nobody complains.*
*VALUE: Inclusion — nobody left out in the rain.*

---

*SCOUT is investigating the puddles forming in the lane with his magnifying glass, despite being completely sheltered by the awning.*

*DOT has a dry page in her sketchbook (she keeps it in a waterproof pocket). She draws the scene — everyone under the awning, the rain, the general chaos. She shows Bea. Bea laughs.*

*EMOTION: The shared laugh is a turning point. The tension releases.*

*BEA disappears inside. She comes back with a tray of the buns she'd managed to save — slightly damp but intact. She distributes them.*
*VALUE: Sharing what survived — not hoarding it.*

*FINN produces a small folding umbrella from his tool belt. He opens it over GERTA TORTOISE, who has appeared from somewhere and is making her way very slowly down the lane.*
*VALUE: Respect for elders and consideration for others.*

*Gerta reaches the awning. She is completely dry. She nods at Finn. He nods back.*

---

*The rain begins to ease. Then stops.*

*Brilliant sunshine pours through the clearing clouds.*

*Everything glistens. The lane sparkles. A rainbow appears over the canopy.*

*EMOTION: Everyone — collective exhale, heads tilting up, eyes closing in the warm returning sun. That particular peace after a storm.*

---

## BEAT 5 — PAYOFF & QUIET (11:30–15:00)

*Everyone spreads back out. The crisis is over.*

*— Ruby surveys the laundry. Half of it is actually fine — lighter things dried fast in the warm sun. She's re-hanging the wet things, and RIVER appears from next door and holds them up high on the line without being asked (River is tall enough).*
*VALUE: Helping others.*

*— DAX goes to his painting. It survived perfectly under the awning. He holds it up to the light and looks at it. Then he looks at Bea's bakery. He picks up a brush.*
*Next episode, there will be a small painting of the Honey Loaf Bakery in his front window.*
*VALUE: Gratitude expressed through art — a small detail kids who watch again will notice.*

*— FINN's parts are all safe under his smock. He inspects each one. He looks at Dax's ruined smock. He takes it inside. He comes back with a clean new one and hangs it on Dax's easel.*
*VALUE: Replacing what someone sacrificed for you.*

*— SPROUT toddles out and jumps in every puddle. She gets completely wet again. Nobody is surprised.*

*Wide shot: Wobbly Hollow after the rain. Rainbow fading. Everything bright and clean. Smoke curling from the bakery chimney.*

*FADE OUT.*
