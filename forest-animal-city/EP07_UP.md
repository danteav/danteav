# TOPPLE TOWN
## Episode 7: "Up"
**Runtime:** 10–15 minutes
**Lead family:** The Dawnleys (River + Fern) + The Berrymores (Bella)
**No dialogue. No words. Pure visual storytelling.**
**Emotion arc:** Love → Horror → Determination → Fear → Courage → Teamwork → Relief → Love
**Values:** Perseverance · Helping Others · Trust · Consideration · Courage

---

### THE OBSTACLE
Bella Berrymore's beloved stuffed squirrel "Nutsy" gets carried up into the canopy by a gust of wind and becomes lodged high in a tree — far beyond anyone's reach. River immediately volunteers to climb for it, but the tree is higher and more dangerous than any River has climbed before. Fear arrives. And for once, River has to accept help rather than go it alone.

---

## BEAT 1 — SETUP (0:00–1:30)

**EXT. WILLOWBROOK PARK — AFTERNOON**

*Bright and breezy. Bella sits on the park bench with Nutsy, showing the stuffed squirrel the view — the creek, the kite-flyers, the ducks. It's a perfect afternoon.*

*EMOTION: Gentle, quiet love. Bella holds Nutsy up to "see" things.*

*A strong gust of wind hits. Bella's ears fly back. And Nutsy—*

*—is ripped from her hands.*

*Nutsy tumbles upward in the gust — cartwheeling through the air — and lodges in the fork of a very large, very tall tree at the edge of the park. About forty feet up.*

*Bella stands. She watches where Nutsy went. Her face changes.*

*EMOTION: The particular horror of watching something precious go somewhere unreachable. Lower lip wobbling. Not crying yet — just the terrible stillness before.*

*She looks at the tree. Forty feet. She looks at her own small paws.*

*She looks up at the tree again. Nutsy is clearly visible, caught in the fork.*

---

## BEAT 2 — ATTEMPT & FAIL (1:30–5:00)

**BELLA TRIES**

*Bella approaches the tree. She finds the lowest branch — it's still six feet off the ground. She jumps. She doesn't make it.*

*She piles up rocks as a step. She jumps from the rocks. She touches the branch — can't grip.*

*She finds a long stick. She tries to poke Nutsy loose from forty feet below. The stick is six feet long.*

*EMOTION: Determination fighting reality. She will not stop trying.*
*VALUE: Perseverance — she tries every idea she has.*

---

**RIVER ARRIVES**

*RIVER DAWNLEY drops out of a nearby tree (River was already up there, because River is always up something).*

*River sees Bella. Sees the tree. Sees Nutsy at the top. River sizes up the tree with expert eyes.*

*River has climbed every tree in the park. This is the biggest one. River looks at it longer than usual.*

*EMOTION: River's face shows a flicker of something — not quite doubt, but respect for the height.*

*Then: the default grin. River has decided. River starts climbing.*

*Bella watches with huge, grateful, terrified eyes.*

---

**THE CLIMB — IT GETS HARDER**

*River is a natural climber. The first twenty feet go quickly — confident, sure-footed, branch to branch.*

*At twenty-five feet, River pauses. Looks down.*

*This is the highest River has been. The park below looks small. The people look small. Bella is a tiny brown shape with round ears.*

*EMOTION: Fear arrives. Not panic — River is too experienced for panic. But genuine fear. Eyes going a little wide. Grip tightening. Movement slowing.*

*River looks up. Nutsy is fifteen feet above. The branches get thinner.*

*River looks down again.*

*EMOTION: Fear and determination at war. Neither is winning.*

*River tries to keep going. Reaches for the next branch. The branch flexes under the weight — it's thinner than it looked. River freezes.*

*Thirty feet up. Frozen.*

---

## BEAT 3 — OTHERS NOTICE (5:00–7:00)

*FERN has been walking the park path below. She looks up at the sound of rustling, sees River frozen in the tree, does a full-stop.*

*She walks to the base of the tree. She looks up at River. River looks down at Fern.*

*EMOTION: River — the complicated look of someone who wants help but doesn't want to need it.*

*Fern looks at the tree. She looks around the park. She walks to the creek bank and picks up a long fallen branch — thin but flexible. She returns to the tree's base.*

*She holds up the branch. She mimes: could reach from about twenty feet if River could get to there and I extended this from below.*

*River looks. Calculates. Looks at the branch River is clinging to. Looks at Nutsy. Looks at Fern.*

*VALUE: Trust — River has to trust that Fern's plan works, and that asking for help isn't failure.*

*BELLA is watching from below, Nutsy's absence obvious in her empty arms.*

*BERNIE arrives (he heard from someone who heard). He's too big to climb the tree. He stands at the base, paws up, ready to catch anything or anyone.*
*VALUE: Helping others with what you have — Bernie can't climb, but he can be the safety net.*

---

## BEAT 4 — TOGETHER (7:00–11:30)

**THE PLAN**

*Fern has also called for ZARA (she was nearby). Zara immediately assesses and contributes: she ties the flexible branch to a second branch using lashing from her bag, effectively extending its reach by another few feet.*

*Value of preparation: Zara always has string. Everyone accepts this.*

*From below: Bernie steady at the base. Fern holding the extended branch. Zara directing the angle.*

*River looks at the plan. Takes a breath.*

*River begins moving again — not up, sideways along a sturdier branch, getting closer to where Fern's extended pole can reach the fork where Nutsy is lodged.*

*EMOTION: River moving carefully — the confidence is different now. It's not showing off. It's just getting the job done.*

*River reaches a point eight feet from Nutsy. The extended pole, fully stretched, reaches up from Fern below.*

*River takes the tip of the pole.*

*The pole is not quite long enough.*

*River stretches — leaning out from the branch — one arm around the tree, one arm reaching with the pole—*

*—the pole tip hooks Nutsy's ear.*

*River pulls gently. Nutsy tilts.*

*River pulls again. Nutsy loosens.*

*One more pull. Nutsy falls — a forty-foot drop—*

*—and BERNIE catches it perfectly. Both huge paws, clean catch.*

*EMOTION: A held breath. Then: Bernie holds Nutsy up. Intact. Fine.*

*Bella's hands fly to her mouth.*

---

**RIVER COMES DOWN**

*River descends — more carefully than the climb up. No showing off. Hand-hold by hand-hold.*

*At ten feet, River drops to the grass.*

*EMOTION: River straightens up and breathes. Then lets out a very quiet exhale — the release of fear finally being over. River looks at hands — they're shaking slightly. River looks around to see if anyone noticed.*

*Fern noticed. She doesn't say anything. She just stands next to River.*
*VALUE: Consideration — giving someone privacy in a vulnerable moment.*

---

*Bernie hands Nutsy to Bella.*

*Bella holds Nutsy. She looks at it. She looks up at the tree — the fork where Nutsy was — forty feet up.*

*She looks at River.*

*She walks over. She holds Nutsy out toward River.*

*EMOTION: An offering — not of Nutsy, but a gesture of the greatest gratitude she knows.*

*River gently pushes Nutsy back to Bella. Shakes head. A smile.*
*VALUE: The gift given, the gift returned with love.*

*Bella hugs Nutsy. Then hugs River. River — surprised — hugs back.*
*EMOTION: Love. Simple and complete.*

---

## BEAT 5 — PAYOFF & QUIET (11:30–15:00)

*The group sits at the base of the tree. Bernie has produced honey buns from somewhere (he always has them, this is never explained).*

*— Fern sits next to River. River is still quieter than usual. Fern pulls out her nature journal and shows River a pressed leaf from the tree — she'd collected it earlier. She gives it to River.*
*VALUE: A small recognition — I see you did something hard.*

*— Bella has Nutsy positioned in her lap facing the tree, "looking at" the spot where Nutsy was stuck. Bella gives Nutsy a little wave, as if Nutsy can see.*

*— Bernie offers Zara a honey bun. Zara takes it. She's writing in her notebook. The notebook has a diagram of the pole extension, labeled. She rips the page out and hands it to Fern.*
*VALUE: Sharing knowledge and credit.*

*— SCOUT appears from behind a bush with his magnifying glass. He has apparently been documenting the whole event. He has four pages of detailed drawings. He presents these to the group very seriously.*

*River looks at Scout's drawings — River's frozen moment in the tree is drawn in great detail.*

*River laughs. A real, relieved, everything-is-okay laugh.*

*EMOTION: Joy. The kind that only comes after fear.*

*Final wide shot: the group at the base of the big tree. Nutsy propped against the trunk in a place of honor. The sun beginning to lower.*

*FADE OUT.*
